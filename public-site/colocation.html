Onboarding

Go Live in 4 Simple Steps

From signing up to having your servers online — our streamlined process gets you racked and connected fast.

01

Choose Your Space

Select from 1U slots, half racks, full racks, or a private cage. Tell us your power, bandwidth, and cooling requirements.

02

Ship or Deliver

Ship your hardware directly to our data centre or deliver in person. Our team handles receive, label, and rack staging for you.

03

Rack & Connect

We mount, cable, and power your equipment. Dual power feeds terminated, ethernet patched to your allocated switch ports.

04

Go Live

Your IPs are announced, your servers are online, and you get access to the customer portal for real-time monitoring.

You can ship hardware directly to our delivery address (we accept and log receipt), or deliver in person. We offer a Smart Hands service to unbox, rack, cable, and power your equipment for an additional fee.
